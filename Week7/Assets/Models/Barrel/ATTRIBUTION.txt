Adaptations in this package:
- Selected the original triangulated barrel mesh (barrel_tris.obj).
- Added barrel.mtl and OBJ material links for the current importer.
- Uniformly scaled to height 2.0 and centered X/Z at the bottom pivot.
- Clamped two UVs from U=-0.0014 to U=0 (less than one texture pixel).
- Renamed diffuse and normal texture copies; image pixels are unchanged.
- Selected the supplied DirectX normal map as normal.png.
- Included the supplied OpenGL normal map as normal_gl.png.
  Change `norm normal.png` to `norm normal_gl.png` if your tangent convention requires it.

Keep these files together under Assets/Models/Barrel_ImporterReady/.
Load barrel.obj. The package contains no .obj.bin cache.
The MTL uses relative paths, Kd 1 1 1, d 1, map_Kd, and norm.
The current renderer uses diffuse and normal; other PBR maps are omitted.
If you change the MTL after import, remove its barrel.obj.bin cache and restart the editor.

Validation: triangle faces, all position/UV/normal indices, UV bounds,
material links and texture decoding checked. Engine rendering is not verified.
